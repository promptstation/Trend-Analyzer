## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the complete memetic analysis framework — form (what the meme is), lineage (where it came from), mechanics (how it replicates and mutates), selection (what filters it passes), lifecycle (what stage it occupies), and meaning (what it signals in each community) as the six analytic dimensions; the meme-intelligence workflow — detection of emerging templates, genealogy reconstruction, velocity and remix measurement, lifecycle classification, community-meaning reading, and strategic recommendation; forecasting memetic trajectories — applying course-3 methods to template-instance series with the fast-cycle, high-noise realities; cross-course integration — network analysis (course 2) for spread paths and bridge communities, NLP and sentiment (course 1) for meaning drift at scale, attention economics (course 4) for the competitive frame, and diffusion theory (course 12) for adoption curves; brand memetic strategy — the participation decision tree covering lifecycle timing, community fit, ethics clearance, and measurement plan; case synthesis — worked full-cycle analyses of breakout memes from origination to exhaustion/revival; failure post-mortems — brand, political, and analytic failures decomposed by dimension; the reporting standard — memetic findings communicated with ambivalence caution, attribution credit, and lifecycle-timing advice; tooling blueprint — the tracking stack (detection, genealogy, velocity, meaning) as an operating system; and the capstone — a complete meme-intelligence project: detect an emerging memetic family, reconstruct its lineage and mutation patterns, measure its spread and lifecycle stage across platforms, read its community meanings and selection pressures, assess its commercial or cultural significance with ethical clearance, and deliver a monitored intelligence report.
