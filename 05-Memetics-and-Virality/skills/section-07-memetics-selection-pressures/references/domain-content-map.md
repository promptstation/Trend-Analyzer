## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the filter side: the multi-level selection environment — audience choices (attention, sharing, remixing), platform algorithms (amplification and suppression), community gatekeeping (norms, moderation, subcultural policing), and inter-meme competition for finite attention; algorithmic selection — how engagement-prediction objectives favor high-arousal, quickly-decodable, remix-friendly content, and how this shapes which memes dominate (connecting to the attention-economics course); the selection-for-platforms dynamic — memes evolving traits that please ranking systems (hook structure, completion optimization, sound reuse), an evolutionary response to artificial selection; audience selection — demographic and psychographic filters determining which communities adopt which memes, and in-group signaling as a selection force (memes that mark membership); contextual selection — news events, seasons, and cultural moments as environmental shifts that suddenly favor dormant meme traits (reactivation); community selection — norm enforcement (backlash against misuse, cringe dynamics killing over-adopted memes) and saturation-driven decline; brand and commercial selection — market filtering of memetic marketing attempts; suppression and moderation — platform policy as selection against categories of memes, with enforcement variability as an evolutionary factor; the fitness-landscape metaphor applied — local optima (format conventions), landscape shifts (algorithm updates), and generalists versus specialists; and selection analysis practice — for any meme's trajectory, identifying which filters it passed or failed at each stage.
