Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Build platform-intelligence working models from weighted evidence streams (disclosures, creator knowledge, audits, research).
2. Maintain living platform dossiers with evidence-graded claims and verification dates.
3. Detect and respond to algorithmic drift through audit repetition and ecosystem monitoring, calibrate confidence in model claims against prediction outcomes.
4. Operate sustainable audit and monitoring cadences integrated with the trend stack.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates operational practice: the working-model concept — a maintained, evidence-based representation of each platform's recommendation system (objectives, funnel structure, known ranking factors, exploration behavior, current experiments) treated as a hypothesis set, not truth; model construction — assembling from the four evidence streams: official disclosures, creator-community knowledge, systematic audits, and public research, with source-reliability weighting; the platform dossier — the living document format capturing objectives, signals, amplification mechanics, observed behaviors, open questions, and last-verified dates per claim; drift monitoring — detecting algorithm changes through audit repetition, creator-anomaly reports, ecosystem metric shifts (engagement-rate distributions, format-mix changes), and disclosure diffs; the change-response workflow — when a platform update is detected: verify, assess impact on tracked trends, update the dossier, and re-baseline forecasts; confidence calibration — labeling each model claim with evidence grade (disclosed, replicated-audit, single-audit, creator-anecdote, speculation) and reviewing calibration of past predictions; tooling — the audit scripts, feed-capture pipelines, trend-data feeds, and dossier systems as the analyst's platform-intelligence stack; team knowledge management — sharing platform models across analysts with version control and challenge rituals; integration with the trend stack — feeding platform-intelligence into amplification attribution (section 12), forecast adjustment (course 3), and attention measurement (course 4); the cost-benefit of depth — which platforms deserve audit-grade models versus disclosure-reading, based on the trend question's stakes; and the operating cadence — quarterly deep audits, monthly drift checks, weekly ecosystem scanning as the sustainable rhythm.
