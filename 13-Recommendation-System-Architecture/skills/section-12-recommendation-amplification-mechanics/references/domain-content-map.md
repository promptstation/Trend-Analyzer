## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the joint dynamics of algorithmic and social spread: the amplification question — for any observed trend, partitioning its growth into organic network diffusion (course-2 cascades), algorithmic recommendation, and search-driven discovery, with the decomposition methods (cascade-shape analysis, traffic-source data where available, timing signatures); the velocity-signal feedback — organic early engagement feeding ranking signals feeding algorithmic boost feeding more engagement: the compound loop that distinguishes platform-era virality from pure social contagion; timing signatures — organic cascades show network-shaped diffusion (cluster-to-cluster via bridges), algorithmic boosts show audience-shaped jumps (sudden exposure to disconnected cohorts), and the analyst's discrimination tests; platform-seeding effects — editorial curation, featured placements, and hashtag/sound promotion pages as human-in-the-loop amplification layers above pure algorithmic ranking; cross-platform amplification chains — trends migrating through platforms (TikTok sound to Instagram Reels to YouTube Shorts) with each platform's recommender re-amplifying, and the migration-lag data as evidence; the recommendation-driven trend lifecycle — how algorithmic saturation (audience exhaustion, engagement-rate decay) ends trends faster than organic dynamics would, connecting to memetic lifecycles; gaming and counter-gaming — engagement-farming, view-bots, and comment-pods as artificial amplification injections, with platform detection and the analyst's contamination checks; the supply-side response — creators optimizing for amplification mechanics producing format convergence (the homogenization evidence) as a trend-ecosystem effect; measurement integration — combining audit data (section 11), cascade analysis, and traffic-source evidence into amplification attribution; the attribution honesty standard — claims about algorithmic causation require evidence beyond correlation, with the confound catalog; and the amplification brief — the professional decomposition of a trend's growth sources with confidence levels.
