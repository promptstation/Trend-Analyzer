## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the search-side amplification layer: search ranking fundamentals — relevance (query-document matching from TF-IDF through embeddings), authority signals (PageRank lineage), freshness, and personalization layers, as the classic recommendation-relative system with different objectives (satisfaction per query versus engagement over sessions); Google Trends methodology — how the index is constructed (sampled search volume, normalized 0-100 relative to peak within query/time/geography scope), what it measures (interest proxy) and its documented quirks (sampling variability across pulls, normalization changes, the 2024 data-access shifts) requiring the stabilization practices from the forecasting course; autocomplete and suggestions — the query-completion pipeline as a signal amplifier (suggestion visibility driving search behavior, a documented feedback loop) and as an analyst data source (suggestion mining for emerging queries); related searches and People-Also-Ask — structured query-graph data revealing topic associations; search as leading indicator — the intent-before-action logic making search trends precede purchase and adoption trends, with the nowcasting literature's evidence and limits; YouTube search versus browse — the search-recommendation traffic split creators optimize for, and keyword-to-feed pipeline dynamics; app-store and marketplace search — the commercial search systems where ASO/SEO optimization is recommendation gaming in miniature; SEO as ecosystem response — the content-supply adaptation to search ranking (and its LLM-answer disruption as the current shift); voice and conversational search emerging patterns at overview; the search-recommendation convergence — feed systems incorporating query signals and search incorporating engagement signals; and the analyst's search-intelligence toolkit — Trends queries, suggestion mining, and search-volume forecasting as the measurable layer of recommendation amplification.
