## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated framework — recommendation systems analyzed across architecture (funnel, models), objectives (what's maximized), dynamics (loops, cold start, amplification), observation (auditing, inference), and governance (transparency, compliance) as one intelligence discipline; the algorithmic-intelligence workflow — the full professional process: define the trend question, map the relevant platforms' working models, design the evidence plan (disclosures, audits, creator intelligence, aggregate data), execute probes with ethics and ToS compliance, decompose observed trends into amplification components, forecast platform-mediated trajectory with drift awareness, and deliver with confidence grades; cross-course integration — the complete stack: recommendation amplification (this course) layered over network spread (course 2), memetic evolution (course 5), and attention economics (course 4), feeding time-series forecasts (course 3) with platform-intelligence adjustments, all grounded in demographic (course 10) and driver (course 11) context; the trend-mechanism attribution standard — every significant trend explained by its algorithmic, social, search, and driver components with evidence, the discipline separating professional analysis from platform folklore; case synthesis — worked attributions (a TikTok sound trend's escalation ladder, a YouTube topic wave's search-versus-recommendation split, a cross-platform migration chain) decomposed through the framework; failure post-mortems — the canonical errors (n=1 audit conclusions, correlation-as-causation, stale working models, gaming-the-guess strategies, ToS violations) with prevention practice; the deliverable suite — platform dossiers, amplification briefs, audit reports, and integrated trend analyses as the professional outputs; ethics position — compliant intelligence, honest attribution, and harm awareness (not building manipulation playbooks) as the practice standard; and the capstone — a complete algorithmic trend-intelligence project: select a live trend on a major platform, build or update the platform working model, design and execute a compliant audit plan, decompose the trend's growth into amplification components with confidence grades, forecast its platform-mediated trajectory accounting for drift and saturation, and deliver an integrated intelligence report connecting algorithmic findings to the broader trend-analysis stack.
