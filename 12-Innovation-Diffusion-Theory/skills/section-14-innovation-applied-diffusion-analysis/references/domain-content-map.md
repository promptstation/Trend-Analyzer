## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Applies the full framework to the course's named frontier domains: AI adoption analysis — the current diffusion state (enterprise adoption surveys, consumer feature-embedded usage versus standalone products), the distinctives (capability improving with use via data-network effects, hype-cycle position contested, complement infrastructure building), the segment pattern (developer-innovators, knowledge-worker early adopters, mainstream embedding through existing software rather than new adoption decisions), and the chasm question (whole-product gaps in reliability, integration, governance); biotech adoption — the authority-decision dominance (regulatory approval gates, prescriber and payer mediation), long decision periods, and the mixed optional/authority dynamics of consumer-facing biotech (direct-to-consumer genetic testing curves, GLP-1 adoption's documented speed as a case in attribute alignment); spatial computing — the hardware-S-curve constraints (price, comfort, content ecosystem), the complement-gate analysis (killer-app absence versus productivity niches), historical reference classes (VR waves, 3D TV failure decomposition), and the readiness-hype joint position; historical calibration cases — electrification (decades-long with infrastructure coupling), telephone and mobile (network-effect classics), mobile money (M-Pesa's Kenya-originated mainstream-first diffusion inverting adopter categories and exporting to other markets — the leapfrog pattern), smartphones (fastest hardware diffusion with app-ecosystem coupling), and streaming (replacement-displacement dynamics); cross-case pattern extraction — what determined speed (attribute profiles, decision types, infrastructure needs, network effects, incumbents' response) as the comparative table; domain-specific forecasting — applying analog, Bass, and indicator methods per domain with their uncertainty structures; the diffusion-strategy read — for each domain, who the current adopter segment is, what the next segment requires, and what gates the crossing; and the domain brief format — the professional assessment combining hype position, readiness, adopter-stage mapping, curve analysis, gates, and forecast with reference classes.
