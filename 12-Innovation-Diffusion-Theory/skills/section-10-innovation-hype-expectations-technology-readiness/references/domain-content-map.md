## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the expectations layer of diffusion: the Gartner Hype Cycle — its five phases (technology trigger, peak of inflated expectations, trough of disillusionment, slope of enlightenment, plateau of productivity), its widespread use for expectation management, and its documented limitations (qualitative construction, unfalsifiability concerns, single-cycle assumption when technologies re-hype); hype as diffusion force — expectation inflation's real effects: investment surges funding infrastructure, talent flows, media attention accelerating awareness-stage diffusion — and the bust's cleanup effect (overcapacity, consolidation, survivor advantage); the hype-adoption relationship — media hype preceding adoption curves, with the analyst's use of hype-phase position to time adoption forecasts (trough entry as buying opportunity, plateau as mainstream signal); technology readiness assessment — TRL (technology readiness levels) from aerospace lineage, MRL for manufacturing, and their commercial-adaptation limits; readiness versus product-market fit — technical maturity distinct from market demand validation, with the four-fit framework (market, product, channel, business-model fit) as the startup-stage diagnostic; standards and infrastructure maturity — the complement-readiness gate (5G adoption awaiting devices and use cases, EV adoption awaiting charging networks) as diffusion-rate determinants; expectation-gap analysis — comparing promised capability against delivered performance as the trough mechanism; the emerging-technology diffusion distinctives — long development periods, ecosystem dependencies, and forecast uncertainty making hype-cycle literacy essential for AI, biotech, and spatial computing assessment (the course brief's named domains); historical calibration — past hype cycles (dot-com, 3D printing, blockchain, metaverse announcements) as reference classes for current ones; and the hype-position brief — locating a technology on expectation and readiness dimensions jointly to forecast adoption timing with base-rate discipline.
