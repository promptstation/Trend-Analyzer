## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated diffusion framework — innovation attributes and decision processes (micro), adopter categories and chasm dynamics (segment), S-curves and Bass mathematics (population), network effects and thresholds (structural), hype and readiness (expectational), and rejection and discontinuance (corrective) as one layered model; the adoption-assessment workflow — the complete professional process: define the innovation and its superseded incumbent, map attributes and decision type, locate the adopter-stage position with segment evidence, diagnose curve pattern and chasm/network-effect signatures, assess hype-readiness position, identify gates (complements, regulation, infrastructure), forecast with analog-parametric hybrids and intervals, and monitor with leading indicators and revision discipline; cross-course integration — network structure (course 2) as diffusion substrate, time-series methods (course 3) for curve fitting and forecasting, demographics (course 10) as the adopter-population structure, macro drivers (course 11) as gates and accelerators, and attention and memetic dynamics (courses 4, 5) as the awareness layer feeding adoption funnels; the strategy translation — diffusion-stage-matched recommendations (pre-chasm focus, chasm crossing, tornado scaling, main-street segmentation) with attribute-management and channel plans; the balanced-assessment standard — including rejection, equity, and harm analysis against the pro-innovation bias; case synthesis — worked full assessments (an AI product, a consumer technology, a market leapfrog) decomposed through the framework; failure post-mortems — the canonical errors (category-confusion mislabeling, short-data Bass fits, chasm assumption without diagnosis, hype-cycle single-reading, laggard-blaming) with diagnostic signatures; the adoption dashboard — the monitoring artifact combining penetration series, indicators, forecast revisions, and gate status; and the capstone — a complete innovation-diffusion analysis: select a live innovation (AI, biotech, spatial computing, or consumer technology), execute the full assessment workflow from attributes through adopter mapping, curve diagnosis, gate analysis, and hybrid forecasting with uncertainty, and deliver a stage-matched strategy brief with an adoption dashboard and calibration-scored revision plan.
