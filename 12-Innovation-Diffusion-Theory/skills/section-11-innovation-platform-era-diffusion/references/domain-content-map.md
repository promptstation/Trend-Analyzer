## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers how digital changed diffusion mechanics: the speed shift — documented acceleration of consumer-technology adoption in platform eras (app-store distribution collapsing channel friction, reaching scale in months that took hardware years), and what remains slow (behavior change, trust building, hardware-dependent adoption); built-in diffusion features — platform mechanics as diffusion infrastructure: sharing buttons, invite mechanics, social proof displays, and algorithmic recommendation injecting platform amplification into what classical theory models as word-of-mouth; product-led growth — the freemium-to-paid, self-serve-adoption model as trialability engineering at scale, with the viral-coefficient (K-factor) metrics and their manipulation-prone nature; virality versus adoption in digital — the crucial distinction between usage spikes (content virality, novelty downloads) and durable adoption (retention-based installed base), with retention curves as the real diffusion data (cohort retention as the adoption-honesty metric); platform dependency — diffusion riding on another platform's graph and algorithms (TikTok-sound-driven app installs, marketplace ecosystem adoption) making adoption hostage to platform policy changes; two-sided platform diffusion patterns — creator-consumer and merchant-shopper coupled S-curves with sequencing strategy; digital-division persistence — adoption gaps by income, age, geography, and connectivity remaining substantial despite speed gains, so classical adopter-category analysis still applies within digital categories; the app-store S-curve anatomy — launch spike, decay, and steady-state as the digital adoption signature, with ASO and featuring as channel effects; hardware-software hybrids — spatial computing and wearables as coupled diffusions where hardware S-curves (slow, capital-gated) constrain software adoption; data-network-effect acceleration — products improving with usage (recommendation, AI features) adding learning-curve dynamics to diffusion; and the digital-diffusion diagnostic — separating platform-amplified awareness from organic adoption using retention, cohort, and channel-mix evidence.
