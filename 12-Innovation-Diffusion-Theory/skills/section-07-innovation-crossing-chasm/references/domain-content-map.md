## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers Moore's marketing-adapted model: the chasm thesis — the discontinuity between early adopters (technology enthusiasts and visionaries seeking change and advantage) and the early majority (pragmatists seeking proven, supported, low-risk solutions), producing a stalled-diffusion gap that most technology products fall into; why the chasm exists — the visionaries' willingness to assemble incomplete solutions versus pragmatists' demand for the whole product (the complete solution with services, integration, references, and standards), and the reference-customer dynamics (pragmatists buy from vendors their peers already trust); the technology adoption lifecycle — Moore's five-segment market model with psychographic profiles per segment (enthusiasts, visionaries, pragmatists, conservatives, skeptics) and their differing buying criteria; the D-Day analogy strategy — focusing all resources on a single niche beachhead segment to win a reference-able installed base, then expanding to adjacent segments (the bowling-alley strategy); whole-product management — orchestrating complements, partners, and services to close the solution gap for pragmatists; the tornado and main-street phases — post-chasm hypergrowth (standards battles, share-land-grab) and mature-market dynamics (segmentation, differentiation), completing the lifecycle map; the chasm's evidence status — practitioner-derived, widely influential, and context-dependent: some categories show no chasm (consumer viral products, mandated technologies), making chasm assessment a diagnosis not an assumption; applicability tests — where the chasm model fits (B2B technology, complex consumer tech, enterprise software) versus where other dynamics dominate (fashion, content, platform-consumer apps); the analyst's use — diagnosing stalled adoption curves for chasm signatures (enthusiast-heavy customer base, reference deficit, whole-product gaps) and prescribing segment strategy; and integration with Rogers — Moore's segments as psychographic refinements of adopter categories with marketing strategy attached.
