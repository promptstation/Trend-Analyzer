## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Connects semiotics to forecasting directly: meaning migration as the trend mechanism — trends as large-scale shifts in what signs connote and which codes dominate (the documented arcs: sustainability from fringe-moral to mainstream-status code, wellness from alternative to luxury), and the analyst's task of detecting migrations early; the connotation lifecycle — how sign-meanings emerge (subcultural innovation), diffuse (broker and media translation), saturate (over-use diluting distinction value), and invert or die (irony flip, cringe marking), parallel to the subculture lifecycle at sign level; semiotic leading indicators — the earliest detectable meaning shifts: new sign combinations (hybrid aesthetics), code violations becoming conventions (norm-breaking normalized), new naming events ( '-core' coinage), palette and typography migrations across design corpora, and casting/setting shifts in commercial imagery; the semiotic square as forecasting tool — mapping a category's meaning space and identifying tension points where new positions will emerge (contradiction resolution as innovation engine); trend-terminology semiotics — the words trends get named ('quiet luxury', 'mob wife aesthetic') as crystallization events accelerating diffusion; cultural tension analysis — the deep oppositions generating trend waves (digital-analog tension driving both Y2K-tech nostalgia and analog-revival authenticity, security-freedom tensions driving aesthetics of both retreat and rebellion); forecasting method — combining tension analysis, lifecycle staging of current codes, leading-indicator monitoring, and scenario construction for meaning-space futures; backtesting semiotic forecasts — checking past predictions against observed migrations as calibration practice; limits and humility — semiotics forecasts directions and forms of meaning change, not timing or market size, and integrates with quantitative forecasting (course 3) for those; and the trend-semiotics report — documenting code shifts, evidence, forecast scenarios, and monitoring triggers.
