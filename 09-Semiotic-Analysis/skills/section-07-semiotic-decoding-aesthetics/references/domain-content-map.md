## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Applies the full toolkit to the aesthetics trends are named for: the aesthetic as sign system — defining an aesthetic analytically (its paradigm inventory of visual, material, and behavioral signs; its syntagmatic combination rules; its connotative themes; its oppositional boundaries against rival aesthetics); worked decoding, cottagecore — pastoral signs (florals, gingham, baking, gardens, soft light), connotative cluster (nostalgia, slowness, nature-return, pre-industrial simplicity), myth layer (naturalized escape from digital burnout), and boundary oppositions (versus cyber-gothic, versus hustle aesthetics); worked decoding, cyberpunk — signs (neon, chrome, rain, dense urbanism, body modification, CRT and glitch), connotative cluster (high-tech-low-life, corporate dystopia, hybrid identity), its fiction-to-fashion pipeline, and current revival dynamics; the '-core' system as taxonomy technology — how naming creates categories (Aesthetics Wiki documentation culture, the suffix as genre marker) and what naming does semiotically (crystallization, searchability, dilution); aesthetic families mapped — dark academia (scholarly-gothic signs, classed knowledge connotations), Y2K revival (chrome, denim, optimism-nostalgia), gorpcore (technical-outdoor gear as status), quiet luxury (fabric-indexical wealth signals, anti-logo symbolism), and their oppositional structure as a meaning space; aesthetic evolution mechanics — element drift, hybridization (cottagegoth), irony inversion, and saturation-death as semiotic processes; platform-aesthetic interplay — how TikTok and Pinterest formats shape which aesthetics can propagate (moodboard-friendly, costume-performable, hashtag-nameable); aesthetic competence — who can read and wear an aesthetic credibly (connecting to subcultural capital); trend translation — reading aesthetics for commercial applications (product design, retail space, campaigns) without flattening their meaning structure; and the deliverable — the aesthetic decode sheet as a reusable analysis format.
