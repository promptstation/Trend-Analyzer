## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce

2. Interpret material-culture objects (home, tech, food) with the same methods.
3. Read wear-index and vintage-double-connotation authenticity strategies.
4. Trace fashion trends from runway through street to mass translation identifying which signs survive dilution.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Extends analysis to dress and objects: the fashion system as semiotic machine — Barthes' insight that fashion magazines write clothing into meaning, updated for the platform era (outfit posts, GRWM videos, and haul content as fashion-writing); dress as sign system — the garment paradigms (silhouettes, fabrics, colors, details), outfit syntax (combination rules and their violations as statements), and occasion codes (dress codes as explicit codebooks); fashion codes and their connotations — documented examples (denim's working-class-to-universal arc, the suit's power coding and its casualization, sneaker culture's indexical authenticity economies); material culture — objects as signs beyond fashion (home aesthetics, tech-device styling, food presentation) with the same paradigm-syntagm logic; consumption signs — brands worn as symbols, logo visibility cycles (logomania versus quiet luxury as opposed symbol strategies), and counterfeit dynamics as semiotic crime; wear-and-use indices — patina, distressing, and wear as indexical authenticity signs (pre-distressed products buying the index); body semiotics — grooming, modification, and presentation as sign layers interacting with dress; vintage and revival semiotics — period garments carrying doubled connotation (their era's meanings plus nostalgia-frame meanings), explaining revival aesthetics; trend-cycle semiotics — how connotative saturation drives the fashion cycle (signs exhaust distinction value and are replaced), connecting to the subculture course; wardrobe-as-identity — the personal style system as syntagmatic self-narrative; and practice — decoding a fashion trend's sign system from runway to street-style to fast-fashion translation, tracking which signs survive dilution.
