## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the spatial dimension: the urbanization transition — the global shift past majority-urban (2007/2008 per UN), projected trajectories (approaching two-thirds urban by 2050), and the urbanization-S-curve logic (fastest growth at middle levels, slowing at high levels); megacities and city systems — the largest urban agglomerations, their growth dynamics concentrated in Asia and Africa, and the analyst's scale layers (megacity, secondary city, peri-urban, rural) each with distinct trend profiles; internal migration patterns — rural-urban flows dominating in developing regions, suburbanization and counterurbanization cycles in developed ones, and amenity/remote-work migration as a recent reshuffling with contested durability; city economics of trends — density as idea-mixing infrastructure (the documented urban innovation premium), cities as trend origination points with diffusion to peripheries, and neighborhood-level micro-geography of cool (the subculture-course connection); urban Africa and Asia specifics — informal settlements and informal economies as dominant urban forms (majority-urban-Africa projections), youth-concentrated cities (Nairobi, Lagos, Kinshasa as trend engines), and infrastructure constraints shaping consumption; the rural-urban value gradient — documented attitude differences and their convergence dynamics; suburban and exurban growth — the largest absolute growth in many countries, and suburbia's under-studied trend role; secondary-city rise — growth shifting to smaller cities as megacities saturate, with cost-driven dispersion; climate and geography — climate migration pressures, water constraints on city growth, and coastal concentration risks as trend-relevant shocks; data practice — UN World Urbanization Prospects, national urban definitions' incomparability (what counts as 'urban' varies), and satellite-night-lights proxies; and the geographic trend map — layering population growth, age structure, urbanization, and income by place to locate trend markets.
