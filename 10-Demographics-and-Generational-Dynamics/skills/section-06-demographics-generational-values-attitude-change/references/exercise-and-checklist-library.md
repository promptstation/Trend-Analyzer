# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Apply the Inglehart-Welzel value-axes framework with its criticisms
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Demographics and generations scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** apply the Inglehart-Welzel value-axes framework with its criticisms, grade evidence on documented cohort value patterns (climate, identity, institutions, work) separating cohort from period effects, navigate WVS, Pew, and regional barometer data with comparability cautions.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
