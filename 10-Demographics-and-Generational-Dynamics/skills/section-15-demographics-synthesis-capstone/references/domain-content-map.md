## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the complete demographic framework consolidated — processes (fertility, mortality, migration), structure (age, household, geography), cohorts (generations, values, purchasing power), and forecasts (cohort-component, probabilistic, scenarios) as one analytic system; the demographic trend-analysis workflow — question definition, data assembly with quality assessment, structural analysis (pyramids, cohorts, households, geography), cohort-value and purchasing-power profiling, projection with uncertainty, integration with fast-signal layers, and strategy translation; the five-discipline standard — precise measures, age-period-cohort clarity, regional disaggregation, uncertainty communication, and determinism avoidance, as the audit checklist for any demographic claim; cross-course integration — demographics grounding netnographic communities (course 7), subculture lifecycles (course 8), diffusion curves (course 12), and forecast models (course 3) in population reality; case synthesis — worked demographic-trend analyses (the African youth-consumer window, East Asian aging markets, Millennial housing-driven consumption shifts, Gen Alpha's AI-native entry) decomposed into method components; failure post-mortems — the classic errors (generational essentialism in a client deck, single-variant projection presented as prediction, ignoring sub-national variance, age-period confounding) with their diagnostic signatures; the demographic watchlist — monitoring revision cycles (UN WPP biennial, national censuses, DHS rounds), leading demographic indicators (fertility surprises, migration policy shifts), and their trend implications; communication craft — presenting demographic futures to strategists (pyramid animations, cohort-flow stories, scenario tables) with calibrated confidence; and the capstone — a complete demographic trend analysis: select a market and trend question, assemble and quality-assess multi-source demographic data, analyze structure and cohort dynamics with age-period-cohort discipline, produce probabilistic projections with momentum decomposition, integrate with values and purchasing-power evidence, audit headwinds and tailwinds, and deliver a strategy-ready demographic trend memo with uncertainty-communicated timing windows and geographic priorities.
