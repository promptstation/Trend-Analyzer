## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the attention-analysis framework consolidated — supply (content design, creators), demand (audience attention budgets, cycles), allocation (platform algorithms, curation), and value (brand fame, conversion, civic agenda) as one system; attention audits — the structured assessment of a brand's, topic's, or movement's attention position: current share, velocity, quality, efficiency (attention per spend), and risk exposure; the attention dashboard — combining share-of-attention, velocity/acceleration, decay estimates, competitive benchmarks, and platform-health indicators into one operating view with alert thresholds; attention strategy design — allocating budget and content effort across platforms, formats, and long-short horizons using measured attention economics rather than habit; forecasting attention — applying course-3 methods to attention series with the domain's measurement quirks; case synthesis — worked analyses of attention captures (breakout brands, viral movements, creator explosions) decomposed into mechanism components; failure post-mortems — attention strategies that backfired (trend-jacking, engagement bait, mis-measured campaigns) and their diagnostic lessons; cross-course integration — network structure (course 2) determining spread paths, NLP (course 1) qualifying attention sentiment, and memetics (course 5) explaining content evolution; the reporting standard — attention findings communicated with proxy-bias honesty and decision hooks; and the capstone — a complete attention-economy analysis: define the competitive attention market, build a cross-platform measurement system with normalization and bias documentation, diagnose lifecycle position and velocity, forecast attention trajectories with uncertainty, and deliver a strategy-relevant attention report.
