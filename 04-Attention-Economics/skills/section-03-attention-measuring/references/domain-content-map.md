## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches the measurement craft: the metric hierarchy — impressions and reach (opportunity to see), views and starts (selection), dwell time and completion (sustained attention), engagement actions (active processing), and recall/sentiment measures (depth) — with each level's construct validity and failure modes; viewability standards and the ad-tech measurement stack (IAB/MRC lineage, third-party verification) at working level; attention metrics proper — the Lumen/Adelaide-lineage research combining eye-tracking, facial coding, and behavioral signals into attention indices, and what they reveal about low-attention impressions; platform-native analytics — what each major platform reports (watch time, average view duration, retention curves, reach breakdowns) and its quirks; retention curves as the deepest public signal — reading shape (hook, dip, plateau) for content diagnosis; engagement-rate construction — denominator choices (per impression, per follower, per reach) and why they change conclusions; passive versus active attention and the lurker problem; survey and panel measures — brand-attention recall, share-of-attention panels, and their sample biases; the manipulability audit — bots, engagement pods, autoplay inflation, and clickbait as measurement threats; and building an attention-measurement plan — matching metrics to questions with explicit proxy-bias documentation.
