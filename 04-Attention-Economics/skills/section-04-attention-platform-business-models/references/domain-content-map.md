## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers how platforms monetize attention: the advertising-funded model — CPM/CPC/CPA pricing structures, auction mechanics (second-price lineage, quality-adjusted bids), and why platforms maximize expected revenue per impression rather than raw time; the engagement-optimization layer — how ad systems need sustained attention, aligning platform objectives with time-on-feed up to saturation and backlash limits; subscription and hybrid models — ad-free tiers, creator subscriptions, and what paying changes about attention incentives; commerce models — affiliate, shopping integration, and attention-to-purchase funnels; take-rates and value capture — where money accrues across the chain (platform versus creator versus brand) and creator-economics data; data as the second currency — behavioral targeting's dependence on attention traces, and privacy changes (ATT, cookie deprecation) reshaping measurement; the marketplace structure — oligopoly concentration of digital ad spend, network effects, and switching costs; platform governance as attention regulation — content policies, demonetization, and shadow-ban debates; the platform's attention-allocation power — ranking determines who gets seen, making platforms de facto media gatekeepers at unprecedented scale; and implications for trend analysts — attention flows follow revenue incentives, so model the incentives to anticipate allocation shifts.
