## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the supply-side craft: the first-seconds problem — hook design for short video (visual pattern interrupts, open loops, immediate stakes), thumbnail and title economics for click-through, and the documented platform bias toward click-then-retain (clickbait gets suppressed when retention disappoints); format-native attention — how each format (short video, long video, image carousels, text threads, audio, livestream) structures attention differently, with completion-rate and dwell patterns per format; pacing and information density — cut rates, narrative compression, and the retention-curve signatures of good pacing; platform-specific conventions as attention technology — TikTok sounds and trends, YouTube chapters and end screens, Instagram Stories taps — and why convention-following earns algorithmic favor; seriality and formats that build habitual attention — episodic structures, recurring series, cliffhangers; packaging versus substance — the honest frame that packaging determines selection while substance determines retention and loyalty; A/B and iterative testing culture — testing hooks, thumbnails, and lengths with platform tools; attention design ethics preview — dark patterns, rage-bait, and outrage optimization as degenerate strategies with brand and platform consequences; cross-format adaptation — repurposing one asset across platforms with format-native edits; and the diagnostic loop — reading retention analytics to fix specific attention leaks.
