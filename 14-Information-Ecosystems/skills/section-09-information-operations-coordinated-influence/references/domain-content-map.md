## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the organized-actor threat layer: information operations defined — coordinated campaigns to shape perceptions and behavior, spanning state, commercial, political, and hybrid actors, with the influence-operations taxonomy (censorship operations, amplification operations, persuasion operations, harassment operations); historical framing — propaganda lineage through computational propaganda (the Oxford Internet Institute lineage documenting state-organized social-media manipulation across dozens of countries); the kill-chain model — operations analyzed as target selection, asset building (accounts, personas, infrastructure), content production, seeding, amplification, and objective realization, with detection points at each stage; documented case patterns — state influence operations from platform disclosures (Internet Research Agency lineage, Chinese and Iranian operations, the documented takedown datasets), election interference cases, and commercial disinformation-for-hire industry (the mercenary operations uncovered by researchers); censorship and flooding — the flood-based censorship strategy (drowning signal in noise) alongside suppression tactics, and information-laundering chains (state media to proxies to mainstream pickup); commercial operations — fake-review farms, reputation attacks, SEO manipulation, and astroturf campaigns as the market-adjacent threat slice most relevant to brand trend analysis; hack-and-leak operations — genuine information weaponized (the malinformation category) with timing and amplification coordination; the evidence hierarchy — platform disclosures (self-interested but data-rich), academic analyses (independent but access-limited), investigative journalism (context-rich), and government attribution (authoritative but politicized) with triangulation requirements; attribution practice — technical, linguistic, behavioral, and incentive evidence with confidence frameworks (the intelligence-community standards adapted); and the operations brief — assessing a detected or suspected operation's actor, objective, tactics, scale, and market/trend impact.
