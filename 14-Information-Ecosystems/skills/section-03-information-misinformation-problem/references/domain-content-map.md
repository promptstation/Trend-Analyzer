## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Builds precise problem knowledge: the information-disorder framework deepened — the three disorders' distinctions (falsity, intent, harm) with the seven types of problematic content (satire/parody, false connection, misleading content, false context, impostor content, manipulated content, fabricated content) as the working classification; cheapfakes versus deepfakes — the documented finding that cheap manipulation (recycled photos, false context, cropping) vastly predates and currently outweighs AI-generated content, correcting the tech-determinist expectation; misinformation base rates — the empirical scale findings (false-news exposure concentrated in small high-consumption segments per the exposure studies; most people encounter less misinformation than discourse suggests, while specific communities concentrate it); domain taxonomy — health misinformation (vaccines, treatments), political misinformation (elections, candidates), financial misinformation (scams, pump-and-dumps), commercial misinformation (fake reviews, false product claims), and science/climate misinformation, each with distinct producers, channels, and harm mechanisms; the falsehood-spread advantage — Vosoughi-Roy-Aral's finding that false news spread farther, faster, and more widely than true (novelty and emotion as mechanisms), with the interpretation debates (retweet cascades on one platform, not all information) properly scoped; rumor versus misinformation versus conspiracy — related constructs distinguished (rumor as unverified circulating claim, conspiracy belief as explanatory style); the supply-side map — error-driven, profit-driven (ad-revenue scam operations), ideology-driven, and state-driven production with different lifecycles and counters; harm taxonomy — individual (health, financial), communal (targeted-group harassment, public-order), and institutional (trust erosion) harms; the trend-analyst's exposure — how each misinformation domain distorts trend signals in its market (health trends, political consumption, financial products, brand reputations); and the measurement preview — detecting and quantifying each form as later sections' task.
