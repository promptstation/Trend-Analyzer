## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the private-governance layer: content moderation defined — platforms' rule-making, enforcement, and appeals over user content, as the largest private speech-governance system in history; the moderation stack — policy (community standards), detection (automated systems from section 7), review (human moderators, often outsourced with documented labor conditions), enforcement (removal, labels, reach-reduction/deamplification, account action), and appeals; the deamplification turn — borderline-content strategies reducing distribution without removal, their transparency problems (shadow-ban realities versus claims), and trend-analysis implications (reach suppression invisible in content data); enforcement-at-scale realities — billions of pieces of content, automated-first processing with precision-recall trade-offs, and the documented error patterns (over-enforcement on marginalized speech, under-enforcement on non-English content per the disclosed gaps and leaked audits); transparency reporting — platform enforcement reports, the Meta Oversight Board's case law, and disclosure limits; policy variance — how rules differ by jurisdiction and enforcement capacity, producing geographic inequality in ecosystem quality; the Trust & Safety profession — the industry function's practices, its knowledge as analyst resource, and its post-2023 shrinkage documented across platforms with governance-quality concerns; moderation's trend effects — enforcement shapes which content communities can organize around (demonetization and deplatforming as trend-killers, the documented deplatforming-effectiveness evidence: audience reductions with migration residues); the free-speech-governance tension — legitimacy debates over unelected platform power, and the state-regulation response previewed; crisis moderation — election and conflict surge operations with their documented strains; and the governance-analysis skill — reading platform policies, enforcement data, and governance changes as ecosystem-structure variables affecting trend dynamics.
