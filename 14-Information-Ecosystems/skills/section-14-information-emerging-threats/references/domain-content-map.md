## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the frontier: generative-AI content floods — the cost collapse of text, image, video, and audio production, documented early impacts (AI-slop in search and feeds, synthetic product reviews, SEO-farm content), and the abundance-scenarios for information ecosystems (filter-value rise, provenance premium, trust collapse dynamics); synthetic personas and AI-operated accounts — LLM-driven influence operations (documented early takedowns of AI-run persona networks), their scaling economics versus detection surfaces (stylistic uniformity, behavioral artifacts), and the arms-race projections; deepfakes maturing — political deepfake incidents (the documented election-season cases), fraud applications (voice-clone scams' measured growth), and pornographic abuse as the highest-volume harm per the research; detection under generation — classifier fragility against improving generators, the shift toward provenance-based approaches (C2PA adoption, watermarking regimes and their removal vulnerabilities, platform AI-labeling policies) as the structural response; the dead-internet debate — the meme versus the measurable (bot-traffic estimates, AI-content share studies) with calibrated assessment; LLM-mediated consumption — AI assistants and search-answer engines as new gatekeepers concentrating information selection (the citation-and-hallucination risks, the publisher-traffic implications already documented); synthetic public opinion — the emerging threat of manufactured consensus signals (fake reviews, fake comments, fake grassroots) at scale, and its detection from section-7 methods extended; the epistemic-liability shift — when anything can be fake, genuine evidence faces credibility discount (the liar's-dividend dynamic documented in deepfake research) as the deeper ecosystem harm; scenario planning — divergent futures (provenance-infrastructure success, trust-forking into verified enclaves, saturation-collapse) with monitoring triggers; and the analyst's preparation — integrating synthetic-content checks into verification workflows and ecosystem models now rather than after saturation.
