## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches the temporal dynamics: lifecycle stages — creation/origination (the producer event: error, fabrication, manipulation, decontextualization), seeding (initial placement into channels: fringe forums, messaging groups, or mainstream accounts), amplification (spread through sharing, algorithmic pickup, and elite/media amplification — the documented 'elite driver' pattern where journalists and politicians often boost fringe falsehoods into general visibility), mutation (claim drift, recontextualization, merging with other narratives as it spreads — connecting to memetic mutation), saturation (peak visibility, fact-checker attention, platform action), correction (debunking, labels, takedowns — and their documented partial reach versus the original claim), and persistence/residue (continued belief and circulation despite correction, the zombie-claim phenomenon); lifecycle-speed variance — crisis misinformation (hours: the mass-shooting rumor-cascade research showing false claims peaking before facts), election cycles (weeks), and slow-burn health myths (years) with different intervention windows; the amplification-ladder analysis — tracking which channels and account types escalate a claim at each stage as the diagnostic method; cross-platform migration — claims moving from fringe (imageboards, Telegram) through mid-tier (X, TikTok) to mainstream (legacy coverage) with legitimacy accretion at each hop; the correction asymmetry — corrections reaching smaller audiences later (the documented reach gap), and design responses (prebunking ahead of predictable falsehoods, labels at exposure point); lifecycle measurement — event-tracking methods for claim lineages (first-seen estimation, spread-graph reconstruction, mutation coding); intervention-timing logic — where in the lifecycle each counter works best (seeding: moderation; amplification: deamplification; saturation: correction; persistence: inoculation); the analyst's lifecycle instrument — classifying a live misinformation event's stage to predict trajectory and intervention windows; and case walkthroughs — documented lifecycles (a viral false image, an election claim, a health myth) traced stage-by-stage with evidence.
