## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches unsupervised theme discovery: probabilistic topic models — LDA's generative story, priors, and inference intuition, choosing topic counts, and coherence metrics with their limits; NMF as the linear-algebra alternative; the modern default workflow — BERTopic (embeddings, clustering, c-TF-IDF term extraction) and its knobs; topic interpretation discipline — labeling by reading documents, not by top-word lists alone, and stability checks under reseeding; dynamic topic models and topics-over-time for trend narratives; structural topic models with metadata covariates (how topics vary by platform, geography, time); seeded and guided topic models for monitoring predefined themes; hierarchical topic structure at overview; qualitative-quantitative blending — close reading inside clusters; evaluation — coherence versus usefulness versus construct validity; common failures — stopword-driven junk topics, over-splitting, unstable assignments; and integration into trend products — theme dashboards and narrative tracking across time.
