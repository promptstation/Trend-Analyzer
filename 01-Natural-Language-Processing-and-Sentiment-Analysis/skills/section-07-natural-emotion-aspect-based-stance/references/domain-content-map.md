## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches fine-grained opinion mining: emotion frameworks — discrete models (Ekman's basic emotions and their critiques, Plutchik's wheel), dimensional models (valence-arousal-dominance), and appraisal traditions, with the measurement controversy over whether text classifiers can validly detect felt emotion versus expressed affect language; emotion datasets and their annotation problems; aspect-based sentiment analysis — aspect extraction plus aspect-level sentiment (the SemEval ABSA lineage), applied to product reviews and brand feedback ("battery great, screen dim"); stance detection — favor/against/neutral toward a target, distinct from sentiment (supporting a claim versus liking it), with rumor-stance applications; intensity and comparative sentiment; intent and complaint mining for product intelligence; high-arousal emotions (anger, awe, anxiety, amusement) and their documented sharing advantage as the bridge to virality analysis; designing multi-label taxonomies for monitoring products; and evaluating fine-grained tasks where human agreement is low — soft labels and distributional annotation.
