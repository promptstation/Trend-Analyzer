## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Introduces NLP as the trend analyst's core instrument: the field defined and its pipeline — acquisition, preprocessing, representation, modeling, interpretation, and deployment; the text sources that carry public emotional undercurrents — social media posts, reviews, comments, search queries, news, and forum threads — with each source's population, bias, and access profile; linguistic levels (tokens, syntax, semantics, pragmatics) and which trend tasks live at each level; the compressed history from rule-based through statistical to neural and LLM-era methods as working lineage; why text is hard — ambiguity, variation, context dependence, irony — and what that implies for measurement claims; setting up the analysis stack (Python, corpora, notebooks, version control); the measurement targets of trend text analytics — sentiment and emotion, topics, entities, novelty, and volume — stated as distinct questions; and the course map from representation through modeling to monitored production systems.
