## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Apply and fine-tune NER for noisy social text including emerging entities and aliases, link and canonicalize entities against knowledge bases.
2. Build brand mention and share-of-voice monitoring with bias awareness.
3. Extract product and feature signals from review text, and connect entity data to co-mention network analysis within privacy boundaries.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers who and what the text is about: named entity recognition — CRF-era sequence labeling through neural NER, off-the-shelf models' weaknesses on social text (capitalization chaos, novel entities, nicknames), and fine-tuning for domain entities; entity classes that matter for trends — products, brands, people, places, creative works, events; entity linking and disambiguation against knowledge bases (Wikipedia, Wikidata) and the canonicalization problem; alias, nickname, and fan-slang handling (the "mother" phenomenon and brand nicknames as monitoring gaps); brand monitoring stacks — mention detection covering explicit tags and implicit references, share-of-voice computation and its platform-coverage biases, competitive-set definition, and campaign tagging; aspect and feature extraction from reviews feeding product intelligence; event extraction at overview; entity co-mention networks as the bridge to network analysis; person-tracking privacy boundaries — public-figure versus private-individual treatment; and build-versus-buy decisions for monitoring infrastructure.
