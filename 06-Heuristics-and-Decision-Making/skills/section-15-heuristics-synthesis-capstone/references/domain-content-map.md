## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated behavioral model of trend adoption — individual heuristics (availability, affect, representativeness) filtered through social mechanisms (social proof, cascades, group dynamics), accelerated by scarcity-FOMO-urgency, shaped by choice architecture, and expressed in emotional and identity-consistent purchases; the demand-spike analysis protocol — the full workflow: characterize the spike (shape, timing, magnitude), map candidate mechanisms (proof, cascade, FOMO, marketing confound, supply artifact), gather mechanism-specific evidence (social sentiment, proof-signal changes, adoption sequencing, resale premiums), rule out confounds, quantify mechanism plausibility, and forecast persistence (FOMO spikes collapse; genuine-value adoption persists); mechanism-signature library — the observable fingerprints of each mechanism (cascade: sequential adoption following visible leaders; scarcity-FOMO: countdown correlation and resale premium; social proof: rating/badge changes preceding uptake; emotional: sentiment-spike coincidence); cross-course integration — network analysis (course 2) for cascade paths, time-series methods (course 3) for spike characterization and persistence forecasting, NLP (course 1) for emotion and narrative signals, and diffusion theory (course 12) for adoption-stage placement; intervention design — applying choice architecture and ethical influence to amplify genuine trends or damp harmful cascades; decision hygiene throughout — the analyst's own biases audited at every stage with calibration-tracked forecasts; case synthesis — full worked analyses of real spike types (product drops, panic buying, viral adoption, manufactured astroturf); reporting standards — mechanism claims with evidence grades and uncertainty; and the capstone — a complete behavioral trend analysis: take a real or simulated demand spike, execute the full protocol from characterization through mechanism identification and confound ruling to persistence forecasting and ethical intervention design, delivering a decision-hygiene-audited report.
