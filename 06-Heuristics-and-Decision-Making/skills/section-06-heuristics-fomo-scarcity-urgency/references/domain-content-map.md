## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Distinguish trait and state FOMO and their demand-acceleration roles.
2. Analyze scarcity mechanics (quantity, time, drop culture) with reactance-backfire awareness.
3. Detect false-urgency dark patterns against regulatory lines.
4. Identify FOMO-driven demand signatures in spike shape and resale data.
5. Position scarcity as an accelerant within multi-mechanism demand spikes.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the acceleration mechanisms behind purchase spikes: FOMO defined and measured — fear of missing out as trait and state, its association with social-media use and well-being findings, and its role as demand accelerator when opportunity seems ephemeral; scarcity mechanics — commodity theory roots, limited-quantity and limited-time scarcity, the psychological pathways (perceived value increase, competitive arousal, reactance when freedom to choose is threatened); experimental evidence on scarcity claims — what replicates (perceived-value and urgency effects) versus contested findings, with effect-size honesty; drop culture and release mechanics — sneaker/fashion drops, limited editions, waitlist mechanics, and appointment scarcity as engineered FOMO at industrial scale; urgency interfaces — countdown timers, low-stock messages, 'selling fast' badges, and their dark-pattern classification when false; reactance theory — psychological reactance as the backfire mechanism when scarcity pressure is perceived as manipulation; FOMO in trend adoption — joining trends late for belonging, the visible-participation evidence (hashtag surges, challenge joins), and FOMO's short half-life versus durable adoption; measurement practice — detecting FOMO-driven demand signatures (spike shape, social-media mention correlation, resale premiums, rapid post-peak collapse); regulatory lines — false-scarcity enforcement (consumer-protection cases against fake countdowns and stock claims); ethical deployment — real scarcity honestly communicated versus manufactured pressure; and the analysis frame — scarcity and FOMO as the accelerants layered on social-proof and cascade dynamics in a demand spike.
