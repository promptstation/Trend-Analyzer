## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Apply Lincoln-Guba trustworthiness criteria through prolonged engagement, triangulation.
2. Audit trails.
3. Maintain reflexive journals and positionality statements documenting interpretive effects, judge saturation and scope claims accordingly.
4. Write thick-description reports weaving contextualized evidence with analytic narrative.
5. Run a pre-delivery quality audit covering rigor, reflexivity, and ethics.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Instills quality standards: trustworthiness criteria for qualitative research — credibility, transferability, dependability, and confirmability (the Lincoln-Guba framework) operationalized for netnography; rigor practices — prolonged engagement, triangulation across data sources and methods, negative-case analysis, peer debriefing, and audit trails; reflexivity — systematic documentation of the researcher's position (platform fluency, demographics, commercial interest, emotional responses) and its interpretive effects, with the reflexive journal as instrument; positionality statements — when and how to disclose the analyst's standpoint in reports; saturation and sufficiency — knowing when fieldwork supports the claims made, with modest-claim discipline; writing craft — thick-description passages, evidence weaving (quotes with context, not orphan snippets), analytic narrative structure, and the finding-claim-evidence chain; visual and multimodal reporting — annotated screenshots (ethically processed), journey maps, and community maps as report artifacts; reporting formats — academic articles, insight decks, and living community briefs, each with fidelity trade-offs; common reporting failures — exoticizing communities, over-quoting dramatic voices, erasing silence (the lurker majority), and trend-report presentism ignoring community history; peer review and critique — giving and receiving ethnographic criticism; the ethics-report integration — consent, anonymity, and harm decisions documented alongside findings; and the quality checklist — a pre-delivery audit covering trustworthiness, reflexivity, evidence traceability, and ethical compliance.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in Netnography through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.
