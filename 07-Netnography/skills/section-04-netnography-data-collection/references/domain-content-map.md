## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the three data streams: archival data — collecting existing posts, threads, videos, comments, and reactions with context preservation (timestamps, threading, edit history), capture methods (screenshots, manual transcription, permitted exports, and scraping's methodological limits — decontextualization) and the curation of a corpus with provenance records; elicited data — in-community interviews (DM-based, voice, video), informal conversational interviews during participation, and community-responsive elicitation (polls, prompts) with recruitment ethics; observational data — fieldnotes on events, interactions, and atmospheres including livestreams and ephemeral content (Stories, live sessions) that archives miss; multimodal capture — video, audio, images, emoji, and interface context as inseparable parts of meaning, and tools/workflows for organizing multimodal corpora; ephemeral-content strategy — documenting disappearing media ethically and practically; event-centered collection — launches, drama events, challenges, and gatherings as natural experiments; longitudinal collection — repeated sampling to capture community evolution; data volume discipline — qualitative saturation logic versus big-data hoarding, and when to stop collecting; documentation standards — metadata schemas, file organization, and the audit trail from raw capture to analysis; and the collection ethics overlay — consent status, sensitivity flags, and exclusion criteria recorded per data item.
