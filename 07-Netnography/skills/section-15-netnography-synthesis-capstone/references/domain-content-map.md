## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute the complete netnographic arc from question to audited report.
2. Select study archetypes (portrait, phenomenon, comparative, longitudinal) with design templates.
3. Run rapid netnography with scoped claims and explicit trade-offs.
4. Maintain living digital-tribe briefings integrated into trend pipelines, and prevent the canonical failure modes (drive-by, extraction, misreading, unreflexive reporting) through documented practice.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert practice: the full netnographic workflow consolidated — question formulation, site selection, ethics clearance, entry and immersion, multi-source collection, coding and thematic analysis, verification, and reporting as one disciplined arc; study archetypes — community portrait (norms, identity, structure), phenomenon-in-community (how a trend lives inside a tribe), comparative multi-site, and longitudinal community-change studies, each with design templates; the trend-intelligence role — netnography's specific contributions (meaning, weak signals, norm maps, authenticity checks) positioned within the full analyst stack alongside NLP, networks, time series, and attention measurement; rapid netnography — compressed fieldwork for business timelines (focused immersion, targeted collection, scoped claims) with explicit quality trade-offs versus deep studies; the digital-tribe briefing format — a living document capturing a community's language, norms, status systems, boundaries, and trend relationships, maintained over time; collaboration models — briefing quantitative teammates, receiving anomaly referrals, co-designing codebooks; ethics as throughline — every stage's ethical decision points collected into the capstone's documentation; failure-mode review — the drive-by, the extraction, the misread irony, the unreflexive report, each with prevention practices; professional development — building platform fluency continuously, community relationships as long-term research assets, and staying current with AoIR-lineage ethics debates; and the capstone — a complete netnographic study: select a Gen Z or Gen Alpha digital tribe relevant to a trend question, design and ethically clear the study, conduct sustained multi-source fieldwork, analyze with documented coding and verification, and deliver a trustworthiness-audited community briefing whose insights integrate into a trend-intelligence context.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in Netnography through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.
