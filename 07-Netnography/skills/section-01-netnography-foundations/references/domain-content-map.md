## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Define netnography and distinguish its emic, immersion-based logic from metric-driven social research.
2. Select community-level sites (forums, Discords, TikTok niches) as units of analysis.
3. Position participation level on the lurker-to-member continuum with trade-off awareness.
4. Articulate netnography's complementary role in trend intelligence, and frame research questions about community meaning that qualitative fieldwork can answer.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Introduces the method and its place in trend analysis: netnography defined — Kozinets' adaptation of ethnographic fieldwork to internet-mediated communities, producing cultural insight from naturally occurring online interaction; the qualitative-quantitative division of labor — metrics (courses 3, 4) tell you what is happening and how much; netnography tells you why it matters to the people doing it, making it the indispensable complement for interpreting Gen Z and Alpha digital tribes; ethnographic commitments — immersion over time, emic (insider) perspective, thick description of meaning in context, and holism (understanding posts as embedded in community life rather than isolated data points); the internet-research lineage — virtual ethnography (Hine), digital ethnography, and netnography as overlapping traditions with shared fieldwork logic; what counts as a netnographic site — forums, subreddits, Discord servers, TikTok niches, fandom spaces, group chats (with access differences), game worlds, and comment sections as communities; participation levels — from lurker/observational to fully participating member, and the immersion continuum's trade-offs; data types previewed — archival traces, elicited interaction (interviews, prompts), and fieldnotes as the researcher's instrument; why communities beat platforms as units of analysis — norms, in-groups, and shared meaning live at community level; the trend-analyst's use cases — decoding emerging aesthetics, values, slang, and consumption meanings before they surface in metrics; and the course map from design and ethics through data collection, analysis, and platform craft to mixed-methods integration and a capstone study.
