## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches platform-specific fieldwork: TikTok — For-You-Page personalization as an ethnographic obstacle (no shared community feed), workarounds (hashtag and sound niches, creator ecosystems, comment-section communities), duet/stitch chains as interaction data, and ephemeral trend velocity requiring fast fieldwork; Discord — server cultures, channel structure as community geography, gatekeeping and roles, invitation access, and the ethics of semi-private servers; Reddit — subreddit norms and mod governance, voting as visible community judgment, thread structures, and Reddit's API changes limiting archival access; Instagram — Stories ephemerality, comment and DM layers, aesthetic feed curation as identity data, and finsta/alt-account cultures; X and Threads — reply-guy dynamics, quote-post discourse, and networked publics; YouTube — comment communities, premiere livestreams, and long-form parasocial contexts; gaming worlds — in-game observation, voice-chat cultures (access limits), Twitch chat as live community data, and virtual-world ethnography lineage; messaging apps and group chats — the access frontier where much Gen Z/Alpha life happens, with consent-heavy approaches; platform-comparison method — the same phenomenon studied across platforms to isolate platform effects; multi-platform immersion workload — realistic scoping of presence across sites; and the platform-fluency requirement — the researcher's own competence as a precondition of valid interpretation, with reflexive documentation of fluency gaps.
