## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Builds the structural toolkit: walks, trails, paths, and cycles; connectivity and components — weak versus strong connectivity in directed graphs, and giant components; distance, eccentricity, diameter, and average path length with their measurement subtleties in disconnected graphs; trees, spanning trees, and minimum spanning trees as extraction tools; bipartite graphs and one-mode projection — the standard method for building co-occurrence networks — with the artifacts projection introduces (clique inflation); degree and degree sequences; subgraph families that matter — cliques, k-cores and core-periphery structure with k-core as a diffusion-relevant depth measure; triads, transitivity, and clustering coefficients (global and local, and their directed variants); motifs and the sociological reading of triad censuses; matrix methods — adjacency powers as walk counts and spectral properties previewed; core algorithms with cost awareness — traversal (BFS/DFS), shortest paths (Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford scope); and the practitioner calibration of reading results versus reading proofs.
