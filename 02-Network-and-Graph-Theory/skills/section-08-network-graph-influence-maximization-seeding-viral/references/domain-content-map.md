## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches the applied optimization problem and its reality checks: influence maximization formalized — the Kempe-Kleinberg-Tardos result (NP-hard, greedy algorithm with the 1−1/e submodularity guarantee) and its assumptions (known graph, known cascade model); scalable alternatives — degree heuristics, discounting for overlap, sketch-based methods (IMM) — and when simple beats optimal; the theory-practice gap — platform algorithmic curation sits between seed and audience, graphs are partially observable, and cascade models mis-specify real spread; the empirical virality record — most content fails to spread, early dynamics weakly predict cascade shape (Cheng-lineage Reddit studies), and the Watts influentials experiment showing context beats centrality for reach; seeding design matched to contagion type — bridge-node targeting for novel information versus community-embedded reinforcement for behavior adoption; measurement of campaigns — contamination controls, holdouts, and attribution honesty; ethical lines — legitimate amplification versus astroturfing and coordinated inauthentic behavior; and the strategy synthesis — what network position, content quality, timing, and platform mechanics each contribute to spread probability.
