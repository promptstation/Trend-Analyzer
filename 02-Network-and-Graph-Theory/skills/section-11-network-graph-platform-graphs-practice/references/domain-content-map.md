## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the trend analyst's actual network data: graph types distinguished — follow/friendship graphs (partially observable, manipulable), interaction graphs (replies, mentions, quote-posts), diffusion graphs (repost and share cascades with timestamps), and co-engagement/affiliation graphs (co-following, co-hashtag, co-viewing) — with each type's analytic affordances and validity limits; cascade analysis practice — hashtag and challenge propagation (TikTok sounds and dances as participation cascades), meme-template reuse networks, and the structural signatures of broadcast versus viral versus community-driven spread; recommendation-mediation — the modern fact that most apparent social spread is algorithmically routed through feeds, and methods for separating network effects from platform amplification; influencer identification with fake-follower and engagement-pod detection; discourse-community mapping and bridge detection between topical groups; data-access realities — API tiers, deprecations, firehose economics, and vendor graphs; graph storage and querying — graph databases (Neo4j/Cypher at working level) and big-graph engineering; and assembling a platform-network monitoring stack for trend intelligence.
