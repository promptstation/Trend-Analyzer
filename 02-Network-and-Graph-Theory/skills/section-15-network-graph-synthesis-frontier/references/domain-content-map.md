## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: graph machine learning — node embeddings (DeepWalk, node2vec) and graph neural networks (message-passing intuition, GCN/GraphSAGE) for link prediction and node classification, with honest assessment of when GNNs beat simple structural features; link prediction as early-signal detection (who will connect next — emerging ties between communities as a trend precursor); causal inference on networks — interference and SUTVA violations, exposure mappings, cluster-randomized and network-randomized designs, and the homophily-influence identification problem with experimental solutions (Aral-lineage work); simulation as laboratory — calibrating agent-based diffusion models against observed cascades and using them for intervention counterfactuals; combining diffusion models with time-series forecasting of spread trajectories; LLM-and-network frontiers — LLM reasoning over graphs and its current limits, networks of interacting agents; federated and open-protocol networks (ActivityPub, AT Protocol) changing graph access and structure; privacy-preserving network analysis (differentially private graph release) at awareness; and the capstone — a complete network trend-intelligence project: acquire interaction data, build and validate graphs, run community, diffusion, and vulnerability analyses, identify emerging clusters and spread dynamics, and deliver a monitored, reproducible report with causal humility.
