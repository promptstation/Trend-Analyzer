## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the structural layer beneath campaigns: political consumerism defined — using purchase decisions as political acts (Hirschman's voice-exit lineage reframed for markets, Micheletti's political virtualism), and its prevalence evidence (survey-reported boycott/buycott participation across countries — substantial minorities, higher among the educated and young, with stated-versus-revealed gaps); the values-behavior bridge — when ethical attitudes convert to purchases (price parity, availability, habit disruption thresholds, social visibility) and when they don't (the attitude-behavior gap's documented size); identity consumption — purchases as political-identity signals in polarized environments, the sorted-market phenomenon (brands, media, and even locations clustering politically), and its trend consequences; ethical-label infrastructure — certifications (fair trade, organic, B-Corp, cruelty-free) as institutionalized values-markets with trust and greenwashing dynamics; cause-related marketing — purchase-linked donations, effectiveness evidence, and consumer skepticism patterns; the price of values — willingness-to-pay premiums measured (stated-preference inflation versus revealed-preference reality), and segment analysis of who actually pays; political consumption across contexts — how regime type and market structure shape it (authoritarian contexts' consumption-as-resistance patterns, developing-market priorities of price over values), forbidding WEIRD-template application; generational dimension — younger cohorts' higher political-consumption participation with lifecycle caveats; issue-cycling — which values-issues dominate consumption politics by period (labor, environment, identity, data privacy) as a trackable rotation; the demand-shift mechanism — how values movements create durable category changes (plant-based, secondhand, plastic-free) beyond campaign spikes; and the values-demand brief — segmenting a market by political-consumption orientation with conversion-threshold analysis.
