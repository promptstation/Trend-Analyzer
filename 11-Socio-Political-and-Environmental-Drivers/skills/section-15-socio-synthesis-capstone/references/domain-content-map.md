## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Assess macro drivers through the five-question framework (force, channel, audience, timeline, countervailing).
2. Execute the driver-impact workflow from detection to scored review.
3. Combine slow, medium, and fast drivers in layered multi-speed assessments.
4. Produce impact-assessment deliverables with scenarios, triggers, and strategy translation.
5. Maintain calibration through outcome scoring of past driver judgments.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the consolidated framework — macro drivers analyzed through five questions: what force (type, magnitude, duration), through what channel (price, availability, legality, norms, identity, risk), for whom (segments, geographies), on what timeline (lag structure, reversibility), and against what countervailing forces (backlash, adaptation, substitution); the driver-impact workflow — detection (monitoring feeds), verification (multi-source confirmation), assessment (the five questions), scenario integration (signposts and implications), strategy translation (which trends to pursue, avoid, or prepare for), and review (register updates, forecast scoring); the multi-speed model — slow structural drivers (demographics, climate, institutional trust), medium policy drivers (regulation cycles, trade shifts), and fast event drivers (elections, shocks, campaigns) combined into layered trend assessment where fast layers modulate slow baselines; cross-course integration — demographics (course 10) as the structural substrate of political and environmental impacts, movements connecting to subculture and memetic spread (courses 5, 8), opinion and sentiment feeding from NLP methods (course 1), driver time-series feeding forecasts (course 3), and scenario signposts monitored through the attention layer (course 4); the impact-assessment deliverable — the professional document: driver description, evidence, channel analysis, affected categories and segments, timeline with lags, scenario set with triggers, strategy implications, and monitoring plan; case synthesis — worked driver analyses (a regulation reshaping a category, an election shifting sentiment and policy, a movement revaluing brands, a climate signal moving a market) decomposed through the framework; failure post-mortems — headline reactivity, attitude-behavior assumption, lag ignorance, single-country framing, and decorative scenarios, each with prevention practice; the calibration habit — scoring past driver assessments against outcomes to improve materiality judgments; and the capstone — a complete socio-political/environmental driver analysis: select a live driver relevant to a trend question, verify and assess it through the five-question framework with multi-source evidence, build the scenario set with signposts and triggers, translate into strategy implications for specific categories and segments, and deliver an impact assessment with an operating monitoring plan and calibration-scored review schedule.
