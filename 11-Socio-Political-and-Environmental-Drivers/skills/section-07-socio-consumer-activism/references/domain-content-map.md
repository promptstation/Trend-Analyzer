## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers movements reaching consumption directly: boycott mechanics — organized purchase-withholding targeting brands or nations, their historical record (documented successes and the base rate of failure), and why most boycotts fail (participation decay, free-riding, substitution ease) while some revalue brands permanently; boycott success conditions — media salience, simple demand, vulnerable target, sustained organization, and elite amplification per the research; buycotts and positive political consumption — organized purchase-support (fair-trade, local, B-Corp preference), their smaller-but-durable market effects, and the certified-label ecosystem as institutionalized buycott; brand activism — brands taking political positions (the corporate sociopolitical activism literature), the evidence on effects (in-group loyalty gains, out-group losses, the net-depends-on-audience finding), and the authenticity requirements (position-history consistency); woke-washing and purpose-washing — performative activism detection, the documented backlash pattern, and the gap-audit method (stated position versus supply chain, donations, labor practices); boycott terrain mapping — why polarized environments make every brand position risky, and the no-position position's shrinking safety; cancel dynamics — social-media pile-ons versus organized boycotts distinguished, their different mechanics and durations, and the evidence on actual sales impact (often smaller than discourse suggests); state-linked consumer action — government-encouraged boycotts (nationalist consumption campaigns) with their distinctive mobilization power; measuring consumer activism — event data, social-sentiment tracking, sales-impact analysis with confound control, and survey-reported participation (with its overstatement bias); the activism-response playbook — how targeted firms respond (concede, deflect, counter, ride out) with documented outcome patterns; and the activism-risk brief — assessing a brand's or category's exposure to consumer-activism campaigns.
