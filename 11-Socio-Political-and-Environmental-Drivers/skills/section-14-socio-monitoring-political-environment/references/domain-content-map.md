## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Builds the operational layer: the monitoring architecture — the continuous scanning system covering political events (elections, legislation, sanctions), policy signals (consultations, drafts, enforcement), movement activity (campaigns, protests, boycotts), environmental data (climate records, disasters, resource stress), and opinion (polls, trust, salience) as the five feeds; source catalog by feed — official (legislative trackers, regulatory registers, gazettes), data (ACLED-style conflict/protest event data, disaster databases, climate indices, polling aggregators), media (policy press, trade publications), and social (movement and sentiment signals via the NLP course methods) with each source's latency, bias, and cost; event-data methods — coding political and protest events from news (GDELT lineage) with its documented quality issues and proper uses; the signal-triage problem — filtering the firehose by materiality (which events touch your markets, categories, or segments) using pre-registered relevance criteria; latency management — the speed-accuracy trade-off across feeds (social signals fastest-noisiest, official data slowest-cleanest) and the escalation workflow from signal to verified driver; the driver register — the structured log of active macro drivers with assessed magnitude, duration, channels, affected categories, scenario links, and review dates; alerting design — threshold and change-based alerts with fatigue control (the boy-who-cried-wolf failure mode); dashboard and briefing cadence — daily alert stream, weekly digest, monthly driver-register review, quarterly scenario refresh as the operating rhythm; automation and analyst judgment — what to automate (collection, triage scoring, aggregation) versus what requires human assessment (materiality, channel reasoning, scenario judgment); team and governance — roles, source-verification standards, and audit trails for monitoring claims; and the monitoring system blueprint — assembling feeds, registers, alerts, and briefings into the trend organization's macro-radar.
