## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the attitudinal medium drivers move through: public opinion infrastructure — the polling ecosystem (national polls, tracker surveys, regional barometers like Afrobarometer/Latinobarómetro, cross-national programs like WVS and Pew Global Attitudes) as the analyst's opinion-data sources, with mode effects and coverage biases; issue salience measurement — most-important-problem questions, media-attention correlation, and the salience-versus-position distinction (positions stable, salience volatile — the key analytic separation); trust as structural variable — institutional trust's documented long decline across democracies, its drivers (performance, polarization, media fragmentation), and its trend consequences (expertise distrust, brand-trust substitution, conspiracy susceptibility); social mood and consumer confidence — the confidence indices, their partisan and economic drivers, and the documented weak-but-real links to spending (confidence as amplifier not originator); the spiral-of-silence and preference-falsification dynamics — why expressed opinion diverges from private belief under social pressure, and the preference-shift surprises this produces (documented in elections and norm changes); norm cascades — how opinions flip rapidly once thresholds pass (the same-sex-marriage opinion shift as the documented fast case), and the tipping-point logic for attitude trends; polarization measurement — affective polarization indices, sorted identities, and the opinion-environment fragmentation that makes consensus trends rarer; generational opinion divides — the age gradients in climate, gender, and institutional attitudes as the slow-moving opinion structure; opinion-event responses — how shocks, scandals, and campaigns move opinion with decay patterns; the analyst's opinion dashboard — combining salience, position, trust, and confidence indicators by issue and segment; and the caution — opinion data as noisy, constructed, and context-dependent, requiring trend reading over point estimates.
