## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Formalizes the temporal model: stage one, formation — the innovation cluster (small groups solving identity/distinction needs through new style/practice combinations), typical origins (music scenes, art schools, online communities, street cultures, gaming), and formation signals (new vocabulary, style experimentation, venue/platform clustering); stage two, consolidation — norm crystallization, internal hierarchy development, gatekeeping emergence, media-of-record creation (zines, wikis, dedicated platforms), and the community's self-definition against mainstream and rivals; stage three, visibility — broker discovery, media coverage waves (often panic-flavored first, then trend-flavored), early commercial interest, and the gatekeeping-intensification response; stage four, diffusion and adaptation — commercial simplification, fast-fashion and brand absorption, celebrity adoption, algorithmic amplification of the diluted form, and membership-surge of tourists; stage five, mainstream saturation — mass availability, original-member exit or re-innovation, dilution complete, and the signal's distinction-value collapse; stage six, afterlife — the fork: residual persistence (committed remnant scene), nostalgic revival (20-year-cycle re-mining), fragment inheritance (elements absorbed into mainstream grammar permanently — the most common fate), or total death; the non-linearity caveats — stages skipped (aesthetics born visible), reversed (revival before death), parallel (multiple fragments at different stages), and accelerated (platform compression); stage indicators operationalized — measurable signatures per stage (vocabulary novelty, gatekeeping intensity, media tone, commercial density, membership composition) as the detection instrument; duration ranges by formation type — scene-based years, aesthetic-based months, with the historical compression trend; and lifecycle mapping practice — classifying a movement's current stage with evidence and forecasting the next transition's timing and triggers.
