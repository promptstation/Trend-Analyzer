## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Decompose subcultural style into component systems and read meaning by contrast.
2. Perform homology and bricolage analyses linking values to style choices.
3. Identify insider competence markers and micro-signals outsiders miss, document digital and physical style systems with ethnographic inventories.
4. Distinguish identity-committed style from seasonal fashion adoption.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Teaches reading style systematically: style systems — the components (clothing, hair, accessories, music, dance, slang, gesture, digital presentation) functioning as integrated communication, not decoration; semiotic foundations applied — sign, signifier, and meaning by contrast (meaning arises from difference within a system), with subcultural style as a language of distinction from mainstream and rival groups; homology analysis in practice — mapping value systems to style choices (punk's torn clothes signifying negation, hip-hop's gold signifying arrival, cottagecore's pastoral signifying escape); bricolage analysis — identifying recontextualized objects and their meaning shifts; the oppositional reading — style as symbolic boundary (us/not-us) at three levels (dominant culture, other subcultures, intra-group poseurs); visual codes and competence — the micro-signals insiders read (specific cuts, brands, wear patterns, styling details) that outsiders systematically miss, and why misreading them exposes commercial adapters; style evolution mechanics — internal innovation for distinction, external borrowing, and mainstream dilution pressure driving continuous change (the fashion-cycle engine previewed); digital style — profile aesthetics, feed curation, avatar dress, and platform-native visual languages as contemporary style systems; documenting style — the ethnographic inventory method (photographic catalogs, element coding, meaning interviews) as data collection; style versus fashion distinguished — subcultural style as identity-committed versus fashion as seasonal adoption; and the analyst's output — style-system maps supporting both trend decoding and respectful commercial translation.
