## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Studies the internet-native formations: cottagecore — its Tumblr/Pinterest aesthetic-community origins, pandemic-acceleration (2020 lockdown conditions as environmental shift favoring pastoral-escape signals), TikTok video-native mutation (from moodboards to performed lifestyle), commercial absorption (fashion collections, home goods, brand campaigns), and the debate over whether an aesthetic without a scene counts as subculture; vaporwave — the earliest fully-internet music-aesthetic movement, its irony-sincerity ambiguity, corporate appropriation (Microsoft Windows 95 nostalgia ads), and the death-and-fragmentation pattern (vaporwave spawning synthwave, future funk, mallsoft descendants); e-girl/e-boy, dark academia, weirdcore, and the '-core' suffix system — the naming convention itself as a platform-era taxonomy technology, aesthetic-wiki infrastructure (Aesthetics Wiki and its documentation culture), and the churn rate of micro-aesthetics; Y2K revival — nostalgic re-mining of 2000s style by the generation that didn't live it, the 20-year cycle observation, and revival-as-new-formation analysis; the TikTok aesthetic-industrial complex — aesthetics as content formats optimized for algorithmic discovery, creator-mediated acceleration, and the question of whether platform aesthetics are trends or trend-simulacra; comparison with scene-based subcultures — weaker commitment, faster churn, visual-first identity, and platform dependency as the structural differences; digital-to-physical conversion — which online aesthetics generate offline behavior (fashion purchases, event scenes, lifestyle changes) versus staying as content; lifecycle speed measurements — documented weeks-to-months cycles for micro-aesthetics versus years for scene cultures; the analyst's detection targets — naming events (when a style gets its '-core' label), aesthetic-wiki entries, creator adoption waves, and commerce signal emergence; and the hybrid forms — digital aesthetics attached to real scenes (music, gaming, fashion subcultures with online wings).
