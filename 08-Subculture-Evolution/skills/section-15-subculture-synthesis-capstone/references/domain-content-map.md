## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Prepares expert integration: the complete subculture-evolution framework consolidated — formation type (scene, tribe, aesthetic community, tech subculture), lifecycle stage, pipeline mechanisms (amplification, incorporation, dilution), capital and gatekeeping dynamics, and commercial absorption paths as one analytic system; the forecasting task defined — predicting which underground formations mainstream, when, in what diluted form, and with what ethical constraints, using lifecycle theory as priors, signal tracking as data, and case histories as calibration; the forecast workflow — watchlist triage, deep-dive assessment of candidates (netnographic verification, signal scoring, lifecycle staging), pipeline simulation (who are the brokers, what media will amplify, which industries will absorb first), timing estimation with uncertainty, and dilution-form prediction (which elements survive); cross-course integration — netnography (course 7) for ground-truth meaning, memetics (course 5) for element-level spread mechanics, attention economics (course 4) for amplification dynamics, diffusion theory (course 12) for adoption-curve placement, and time-series methods (course 3) for signal-series forecasting; the subculture brief format — the professional deliverable: formation profile, lifecycle evidence, pipeline forecast, commercial translation options with ethical clearance, and monitoring plan; scenario planning — multiple absorption paths per candidate (fast-fashion dilution, luxury collaboration, platform amplification, stall-and-persist) with probability weights; failure modes reviewed — the false positive (stunt mistaken for formation), the timing error (early or late), the ethics failure (extraction backlash), and the dilution misread (absorbing the surface without the substance); portfolio strategy — maintaining a watchlist pipeline across stages so forecasts mature continuously rather than betting on single calls; the humility standard — subculture forecasting's documented base rates of error, and communicating calibrated confidence; and the capstone — a complete subculture-evolution analysis: select an emerging formation, classify and stage it with multi-source evidence, verify ethnographically, forecast its pipeline with scenarios and timing, design an ethically cleared commercial-translation recommendation, and deliver a monitored subculture brief.
